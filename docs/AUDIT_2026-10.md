## Medium

- Tutor: «عاود» handled only in the call screen, «منين جات» nowhere; free chat keeps no current-question state.
- Mastery can be raised by requesting many practice sets; concurrent gradings may overwrite each other's update (read before claim).
- No whiteboard.
- No OCR for exercise photos (always to a teacher — stated honestly in the UI).
- Service worker caches non-OK responses; nothing cleared on logout (API never cached, so low risk).

## Risks and how regressions are prevented

- Grading change could reject answers that passed before: a test runs every numeric question of the curriculum with its own answer, with its unit, and with a unit of another kind.
- Schema changes: additive migrations only (no drop, no rename), applied by `npm run migrate` to an empty DB in tests.
- Each phase: typecheck, unit tests, real-MySQL tests, build before commit; Playwright journey at the end.
- No production data touched; nothing published.
